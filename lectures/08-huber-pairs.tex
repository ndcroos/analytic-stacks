\bd[Huber ring]
A \textit{Huber ring} is a topological ring $A$ that admits an open subring $A_0 \subs A$ and an ideal $I \subs A^+$, such that there exists some finitely generated $A_0$-ideal $I \subs I$, such that $A_0$ has the $I$-adic topology.

\bd[Ring of integral elements]
